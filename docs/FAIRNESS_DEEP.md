## 2. Less-discriminatory-alternative search

Under disparate-impact doctrine, business necessity does not end the
analysis. If a less discriminatory alternative serves the same purpose
comparably well, the original practice remains actionable. So the
question is not 'is the model justified' but 'is there a cheaper-in-
disparity model that is nearly as good'.

Tolerances, stated rather than buried: an alternative counts only if it
improves AIR by at least **0.010** and costs at most **0.005** AUC.

**No alternative reduced disparity by the stated margin.** Read that
with the caveat below rather than as a clearance.

The caveat that belongs next to every number here: baseline AIR is
0.9940, comfortably above the 0.80 screen, so there is very little
disparity for an alternative to remove. The search is demonstrated; the
scenario is benign. A fairness result on a benign scenario is evidence
about the machinery and nothing at all about the model's conduct in a
population where the disparity is real.
